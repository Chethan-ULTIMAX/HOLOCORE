Calibration & reset

Calibration re-arms the gesture engine: it forgets any held grab, resets the smoothing filters and returns the object to a neutral pose. Use it whenever the object feels offset from your hands — for a new user, a moved webcam, or after tracking loss.

How to use HOLOCORE

1 · Show yourself

Press C or click Enable camera. Once you allow permission your live, mirrored camera fills the 3D workspace — the assembly floats in front of you. Press V to shrink the feed back to a corner preview.

2 · Pick a mode

The mode decides what a pinch does, so nothing happens twice by accident. Choose with 1–5 or the mode bar:

  • Select — point and pinch to select a component
  • Move — pinch and drag
  • Rotate — pinch and sweep left/right or up/down
  • Scale — pinch with both hands and spread
  • Explode — slider, or spread both hands

3 · Pinch means press and hold

Touch your thumb and index fingertip: a green ring closes and the pinch engages. The grab records a reference frame, so the object never jumps to your fingertip. Open the pinch to release. If your hand leaves the frame, the grab is released safely instead of sticking.

4 · Explode, then inspect

Drag the explode slider (or press E) to open the assembly from 0–100%. Every component keeps its own highlight, so you can select individual parts mid-explosion and inspect its specification, connections and failure behaviour.

Mouse fallback: drag to orbit, scroll to zoom, click to inspect. Arrow keys move the object, Q/W push it away or pull it closer.
Camera needs a secure origin. Open HOLOCORE over https:// or http://localhost. File:// pages and plain HTTP on a LAN address cannot access the camera.
